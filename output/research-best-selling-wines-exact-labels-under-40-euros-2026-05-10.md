## Best Picks by Category for Trade Buyers

### Best Single Buy Under €25
**Bodegas Muga Rioja Reserva 2021** — Wine Spectator's Value of the Year. Broad availability, 93-point score, consistent across retail and on-trade.

### Best Discovery Buy for On-Trade Lists
**Guímaro Mencía Finca Meixeman 2022** — Spanish Wine of the Year 2024. Wildly aromatic, under €30 even at restaurant margins, tells a compelling Ribeira Sacra story.

### Best White Under €20
**Invivo X SJP Sauvignon Blanc 2024** — 92 points at ~€18. High consumer recognition, strong social media pull, Marlborough quality baseline.

### Best Bordeaux Value
**Château Labégorce Margaux 2022** — 92 points at ~€32–35. The 2022 Bordeaux vintage is genuinely exceptional and this sits right at the price ceiling.

### Best Alcohol-Removed Pick
**Trinchero FRE** (various) or **Freixenet Alcohol-Removed** — both 2026 Growth Brand winners and the most credible options in a fast-growing segment.

## What to Watch

- **2022 Bordeaux vintage** is an exceptional year for Margaux and Médoc appellations at the under-€40 ceiling — buy now before ex-negociant prices lift retail
- **Ribeira Sacra Mencía** (Spain) is the most underpriced appellation relative to critic scores right now — Guímaro is the leading label but secondary producers are following
- **Alcohol-removed wine** is the fastest-growing sub-category in this price tier; it is no longer a novelty but a buying desk responsibility
- **Uruguay Tannat** — Bodega Garzón is the only name most trade buyers know; expect more labels entering this tier through 2026–2027

---

## Key Takeaways & Recommended Next Steps

- **Stock Bodegas Muga Rioja Reserva 2021** — the most universally endorsed bottle in this tier right now; safe for any program
- **Add Guímaro Mencía 2022 to your by-the-glass or discovery list** — price, score, and story all work; it's the strongest conversation-starter in the sub-€30 range
- **Allocate at least one non-alc SKU** — Trinchero FRE or Freixenet Alcohol-Removed are both award-backed; this segment will grow regardless of overall wine market contraction
- **Audit your Bordeaux allocation for 2022 vintage bottles** — Château Labégorce and similar Cru Bourgeois wines at the €30–35 tier offer exceptional vintage quality before prices move
- **Test Bodega Garzón Tannat 2023** as a program differentiator — Uruguay is the most credible new-country story you can tell trade-educated guests at this price point
